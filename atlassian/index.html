Review the Complete Data Flow

Sprint Scope Guard runs on Forge and stores settings and governance records in Forge KVS. Jira content can be present in those records; browser exports create a local copy for the user.

Its optional Slack or Teams integration is a manual webhook test, not automatic notifications. The remediation sends generic test text and a timestamp to allowed endpoints. Forge logs can include installation-site metadata and are subject to customer sharing controls. Consult the security policy for version-specific details.
